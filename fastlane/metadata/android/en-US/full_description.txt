Speak the dialect, get proper characters.

Roar is an Android input method (IME) that turns spoken Cantonese into
correct Cantonese orthography (正字). Saying "m4 goi1" yields 唔該, not
the Mandarin homophone rendering 无该 — and it commits the result into
whatever field you are typing in: WeChat, a browser, notes, anywhere.

How it works — two transcription layers:
- Streaming ASR (sherpa-onnx, on-device) converts speech to text
- An orthography engine (pure Kotlin: homophone mapping, word-frequency
  dictionary, bigram context ranking) turns Mandarin-ised or homophone
  text into ranked Cantonese orthography candidates
- A Compose candidate bar lets you tap the one you want

Privacy:
- Recognition runs 100% on the device. No audio and no text is ever
  uploaded, and there is no account, no analytics and no ad SDK.
- RECORD_AUDIO is used for the microphone; INTERNET is used only to
  fetch the ASR model (~238 MB) on first run or when you open settings.
  Once the model is on disk the app works fully offline.
- The bundled Cantonese dictionary ships inside the APK; there is no
  network dictionary sync.

Status: early MVP (v0.1.0). The engine, ASR wiring and IME are
implemented; real-device end-to-end verification is still in progress,
and only Cantonese is wired up so far. Mandarin and English speech are
recognised by the underlying model but not yet post-processed.
